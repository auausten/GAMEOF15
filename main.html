The Game of 15

Take turns claiming a number from 1 to 9. The first player to hold any three numbers that add to exactly 15 wins. Each number can only be taken once.

Player 10 wins

Player 20 wins

Draws: 0

It's noughts and crosses in disguise. In this magic square every row, column and diagonal adds to 15, and those 8 lines are the only ways to make 15 from three different numbers between 1 and 9.

So claiming a number is the same as placing your mark on its square. Anyone who knows this can play noughts and crosses in secret and never lose.
